# Source and extraction log
- Primary source: Walmart FY2026 Form 10-K, filed March 13, 2026: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/104169/000010416926000055/wmt-20260131.htm
- Financial years end on January 31; FY2026 ended Jan 31, 2026.
- Consolidated Statements of Income: net sales, membership and other income, total revenue, cost of sales, SG&A, operating income, consolidated net income.
- Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows: depreciation and amortization, cash from operations, payments for property and equipment (capex stored as **positive** spending).
- Original filings report all amounts in **USD millions**, except per-share figures.
- Values manually transcribed from the company's 2026 10-K and cross-checked with its FY2026 operating/FCF reconciliation; rounding as reported.
- The 2026 10-K reports historic FY2024 and FY2025 amounts alongside FY2026.
- Revenue minus cost of sales minus SG&A equals EBIT for Walmart because membership/other revenue is included in total revenue.
- Company-reported free cash flow = operating cash flow - capex. This is NOT the same as FCFF used in the forecast DCF.
